# Broomba Ad Scripting

## Free Beta Positioning

Goal: get people to try one room scan, laugh at the result, save useful checks, and come back. Do not sell a subscription yet.

## Hooks To Test

### AI Room Roast

"I made an AI that judges your room so your roommate doesn't have to."

15-25 second structure:
1. Open on a messy but relatable room.
2. Show the Broomba upload screen.
3. Cut to the roast/result.
4. Do the one tiny cleanup action.
5. CTA: "Try the free beta."

### Tiny Cleanup Action

"Take one photo. Get one tiny cleanup mission."

15-25 second structure:
1. Show avoidance/procrastination.
2. Upload one photo.
3. Highlight the single action card.
4. Show the quick win after doing it.
5. CTA: "Let Broomba pick the first move."

### Roommate Friction Prevention

"Before the group chat gets passive-aggressive, let Broomba say it first."

15-25 second structure:
1. Show shared-space tension.
2. Upload the room photo.
3. Show a playful roast that is funny, not mean.
4. Do the tiny reset.
5. CTA: "Try the free beta."

## Tracking

Use one UTM campaign per hook:

- `?utm_campaign=ai_room_roast`
- `?utm_campaign=tiny_cleanup_action`
- `?utm_campaign=roommate_friction`

Track visit, scan started, scan completed, result saved, and return visit. Keep the app experience consistent while testing hooks.
